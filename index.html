Get your Google calendars
  1. On a computer, open Google Calendar → gear icon → Settings → Import & export → Export.
  2. You get one .zip with an .ics per calendar you own. Import the .zip as-is.
  3. Calendars shared with you aren't in that zip: open the calendar's settings → Integrate calendar → paste the Secret address in iCal format into a browser tab to download its .ics.
Get your Apple calendars
  1. On a Mac, open Calendar, click one calendar in the sidebar, then File → Export → Export…. Repeat per calendar.
  2. iPhone has no export. Use the Mac, or on iCloud.com share the calendar as Public, copy the link, change webcal:// to https://, and open it to download.
  3. On iPhone, pick the downloaded files from the Files app when you tap Import.
Editing events
  • Drag an event to move it. Drag its top or bottom edge to resize. Times snap to 15 minutes.
  • Drag on empty space to add an event. Zoomed in, it stays inside one day. Zoomed out, or in the all-day strip, it spans whole days.
  • On a touch screen, press and hold first, then drag.
  • Tap an event to rename it, move it to another calendar, pin it to the countdown, or delete it.
  • ⌘Z undoes. With a mouse, hold Space and drag to pan.
  • Hold F for the lens. Press / to search.
  • Edits are saved in this browser only. Re-importing a calendar replaces changes made to that calendar's events here.
Moving around
  • Scroll or drag to pan. Pinch to zoom.
  • ⇧ + scroll stretches days. ⌥ + scroll stretches hours.
  • Drag the date ruler sideways or the hour ruler up/down to stretch one axis.
  • Trackpad pinch over the date ruler stretches days only; over the hour ruler, hours only. Holding ⇧ or ⌥ while pinching does the same.
  • On a touch screen, pinch sideways to stretch days, pinch up-down to stretch hours, pinch diagonally for both.
  • Double-tap to zoom in. T today, C calendars, G shape, L style, M sound, ←→ pan.
  • Tap the emoji next to a calendar to change its icon. It shows on roomy event bubbles.
  • ⌥-click a switch to show only that calendar.

Imported calendars are saved in this browser only. Re-import a file to refresh it.
